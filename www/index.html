dx

This tool is a random number generator for simulating dice (dx) rolls. Handy if you went to a D&D session but left your favorite dice at home. You have different way for telling how many dice and what kind of dice you would like to roll: you can specify it as a string (e.g. 3d6+2 means rolling three six-sided dice and adding two to the result, simple dice notations are supported only), but you can also set a minimum and a maximum value, or just click one of the roll buttons.
